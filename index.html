Frequently Asked Questions

Do you really answer calls 24/7?

Yes. Summit Ridge Plumbing offers 24/7 emergency service for burst pipes, flooding, sewage backups, and no-hot-water situations — including nights, weekends, and holidays. Just call (208) 555-0142.

What areas do you serve?

We’re family-owned in Boise, Idaho, and serve the surrounding Treasure Valley. If you’re nearby and not sure whether we cover your address, give us a call and we’ll let you know.

Do you repair and install water heaters?

We do both. We can diagnose why your hot water stopped, repair or service your existing tank or tankless unit, and install a new water heater when replacement is the better choice.

How do I know if I have a hidden leak?

Warning signs include unexplained spikes in your water bill, damp spots or warped flooring, musty smells, and the sound of running water when nothing is on. Our leak detection service pinpoints the source so repairs are targeted and efficient.

Will I know the price before you start?

Yes. We believe in upfront pricing, so you’ll get a clear explanation of the recommended work and the cost before we begin. No surprises at the end.

Is there a charge to get a quote?

Use the contact form on this page to request a free quote, or call us at (208) 555-0142 and we’ll be happy to talk through your project.
